Why Static Typing Matters

By Jane Doe — March 15, 2026

Static typing isn't just about catching bugs. It's about a clearer contract between the author of a function and its callers.

A modern type system, used well, doubles as documentation that the compiler refuses to let go stale. When the type lies, the build breaks; when comments lie, nothing breaks until production.
