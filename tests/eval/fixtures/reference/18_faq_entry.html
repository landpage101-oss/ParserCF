How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password from the login screen. Click the Forgot password? link beneath the login button.

An email with a password-reset link will be sent to the address registered to your account. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can only be used once.

If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder. If the problem persists, contact support with your username or registered email address.
